Hello! I go by the alias "90", and I'm a largely self-taught programmer and modder.

Most of my current work so far has been in Java development, primarily centered around Minecraft mods. The most notable
of these serve as extra add-ons to [_Applied Energistics 2_][ae2], a popular long-standing mod which I have also
contributed to and been directly involved with for a couple of years now. These add-ons include:
- [_MEGA Cells_][mega]: Extra large tiers of storage cells, along with other specialised/bespoke storage and
  functionality.\
  [![][dls-mega]](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mega-cells)
- [_Ars Énergistique_][arseng]: Cross-mod integration with the [_Ars Nouveau_][ars] magic mod, including dedicated
  storage and manipulation of its magical "source" substance.\
  [![][dls-arseng]](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ars-energistique)
- [_AppliedE_][appliede]: Cross-mod integration with the [_ProjectE_][projecte] mod, allowing for alchemical
  transmutation of items directly via AE2's own storage network facilities.\
  [![dls-appliede]](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/appliede)

For the long term, I hope to continue broadening my horizons, and eventually dive into other languages and even larger
passion projects outside of my current work. In the meantime, thanks for sticking around!
