Hello! I go by the alias "90", and I'm a hobbyist programmer and tinkerer, largely self-taught from an early age.

Most of my current work so far has been in Java development, primarily centered around Minecraft mods such as
[_MEGA Cells_](https://github.com/62832/MEGACells), [_Ars Énergistique_](https://github.com/62832/ArsEnergistique) and
[_AppliedE_](https://github.com/62832/AppliedE). The majority of these mods serve as extra add-ons to
[_Applied Energistics 2_](https://github.com/AppliedEnergistics/Applied-Energistics-2), a popular long-standing mod
which I have also contributed to and been directly involved with on-and-off for a couple of years now.

For the long term, I hope to continue broadening my horizons, and eventually dive into other languages and even larger
passion projects outside of my current work. Until then, however, thanks for sticking around.
